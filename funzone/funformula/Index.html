One compact equation can generate an enormous family of organic, crystalline, star-like, and architectural forms. Tune the two angular profiles and watch the surface reshape in real time.

r(ϑ) = ( |cos(mϑ/4)/a|ⁿ² + |sin(mϑ/4)/b|ⁿ³ )−1/n₁
The formula is applied once for ϑ and once for φ to build a 3D surface.
